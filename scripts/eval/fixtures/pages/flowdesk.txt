FlowDesk — the task board for small teams (fictional test page)
Tired of losing tasks in chat threads and sticky notes? FlowDesk keeps every task in one board, so nothing slips.
How it works
1. Create a board for your team.
2. Add a task and drag it into Today.
3. Check it off and FlowDesk moves it to Done.
Screens
Board view: tasks in three columns, Today, This week and Done.
Weekly report: a chart of tasks finished per person.
FlowDesk vs spreadsheets: a spreadsheet needs you to update every row by hand; FlowDesk moves tasks for you.
