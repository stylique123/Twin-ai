Maya's Coffee — Morning Blend, 250 g bag (fictional test page)
Most supermarket coffee tastes flat because it was roasted months ago. Morning Blend is roasted the week it ships.
How to brew
1. Grind 18 g of beans medium-fine.
2. Pour 50 g of water at 94 degrees and let it bloom for 30 seconds.
3. Pour the rest slowly in circles up to 300 g.
Open the bag, smell the beans, and pour the first cup straight into a glass mug to see the colour.
Compared with pod coffee, Morning Blend costs less per cup and leaves no plastic.
Only 60 bags in this roast. When they are gone, the next roast is in three weeks.
