# Your canary intake (Mon 26 Oct, about 45 minutes)

Use the normal onboarding on your own creator account. Nothing here is special for the canary; the canary only changes how scripts are written afterwards.

1. **Sign in** to Twin with your own account (the one you will name as the canary).
2. **Pillars:** when onboarding asks what you make videos about, give **2 or 3** topics.
3. **Stories (the most important step):** when it asks for a story, enter **5 to 10 real ones**, one at a time. Each: what happened, in your own words, with the real detail (a number, a place, what someone said). Short is fine; true is the rule.
4. **Products:** add **Twin** (and any other product you sell) in **Products**. Paste the product page link if it has one.
5. **Optional:** add one or two of your own public videos so Twin can learn your voice.
6. **Left out / Used list:** after your first script, open the **Using** list and mark anything that should never be used (**Never use**).
7. **Copy your user id** (Supabase → Authentication → Users → your email → UID) and send it to me. I put it in the canary settings on Wednesday after your "go".
