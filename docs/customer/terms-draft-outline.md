# Twin terms of service: outline (DRAFT for counsel review)

> **DRAFT for counsel review. Not legal advice. Outline only.** Topics counsel
> must cover, with the product facts each depends on.

1. **Parties, eligibility, account.** Who may sign up; one account per person
   (the database enforces one owner per account); age; business use.
2. **Creator content ownership.** She keeps ownership of her uploads, voice,
   likeness, answers and finished videos. Twin needs a limited licence to
   store, process and send them to the processors in the privacy notice, only
   to provide the service. Licence ends on deletion (subject to backups).
   [[COUNSEL: scope, sublicensing to processors, no marketing use without
   separate consent.]]
3. **AI output.** Scripts, hooks, edits are machine-generated drafts. Who owns
   them; no guarantee of results or uniqueness; may resemble others' output.
4. **Review before posting.** She must read and approve every script and video
   before posting; she is the publisher; Twin publishes only on her action.
5. **Product claims and disclosures.** She is responsible for ad/affiliate
   disclosure and platform rules; Twin blocks known banned brand claims but
   that is not a compliance service.
6. **Honesty / no-invention commitment.** Twin's commitment not to invent facts,
   reviews, results or experiences; her duty not to ask it to. What happens
   when a fact she gave is wrong.
7. **Credits.** What a credit buys, when it is spent (on generation), expiry,
   non-transferability, changes to pricing.
8. **Payments and refunds.** Subscription terms, renewal, cancellation, refund
   policy for unused credits and for failed generations.
   [[COUNSEL: statutory cooling-off rights.]]
9. **Acceptable use.** No impersonation, no content about other people without
   permission, no illegal, hateful or deceptive content, no scraping or
   reverse engineering, no sharing logins.
10. **Third-party platforms.** Connected social accounts are governed by those
    platforms' terms; Twin may lose access at any time.
11. **Takedown.** How rights holders report content (takedown-and-deletion.md).
12. **Termination and deletion.** Her right to close; Twin's right to suspend;
    what is deleted and when.
13. **Liability, warranties, indemnity.** Standard limits; AI output "as is".
14. **Privacy.** Incorporate the privacy notice.
15. **Changes, governing law, disputes, contact.**
